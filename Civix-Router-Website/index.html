The Language Barrier Crisis

Citizens submit complaints in Tamil. Government systems operate in English. Result: Massive delays and misallocated resources.

How Civix-Router Works

A four-step pipeline from Tamil complaint to English department queue.

  1. 👤 01

    Citizen Portal

    Submit complaints in Tamil via voice or text.

  2. 🌐 02

    Real-Time Translation

    Tamil → English using Deep-Translator / Google.

  3. 🤖 03

    Zero-Shot AI Classification

    facebook/bart-large-mnli model categorizes intent.

  4. 🏛️ 04

    Official Dashboard

    Auto-route to the correct department instantly.
